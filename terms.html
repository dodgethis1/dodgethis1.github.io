Terms of use

PiNAS Drive Backup is an owner-operated utility for the owner’s personal backup and recovery. This website documents that setup and does not offer a public backup service.

Authorized use

Use the application only with a Google account and storage you own or are authorized to administer. Google access is limited to the permission approved on the consent screen and remains subject to Google’s terms.

Owner responsibilities

The owner manages storage access, credentials, schedules, retention, recovery checks, and deletion of local copies. Successful copying alone does not establish that every file is recoverable; recovery checks provide separate evidence.

Availability

This personal utility has no service-level commitment or guarantee of uninterrupted operation. Google authorization, connectivity, storage, and software failures can interrupt backups.

See the privacy policy for how Google data is handled. Support contact details appear on the homepage.
