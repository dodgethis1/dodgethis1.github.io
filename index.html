PERSONAL BACKUP UTILITY

PiNAS Drive Backup

PiNAS Drive Backup uses rclone to download the account owner’s Google Drive files to an owner-operated Raspberry Pi NAS. Local encrypted recovery snapshots provide additional recovery points.

What it does

  • Reads file contents and metadata from the Google Drive account that the owner authorizes.
  • Downloads files to local storage for personal backup and recovery.
  • Runs scheduled jobs and reports their completion or failure.

Access stays read-only

The Google authorization requests the drive.readonly scope. This backup application does not request permission to create, modify, or delete files in Google Drive.

Personal use

This is the owner’s personal backup setup. It is not a public file-hosting service and does not accept account registrations. This website describes the application; it does not connect visitors’ Google accounts or receive their backup data.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use.

Support

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen, or open an issue in the information-page repository. Do not post private files, account credentials, or authorization tokens in public issues.
